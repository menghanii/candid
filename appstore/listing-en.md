## Description
candid. is a photo app where your friends — not you — fill your feed.

Instead of selfies you picked, see the real you through your friends' cameras. Choose which of their photos make it onto your feed.

■ A feed filled by friends
· Send photos, videos, polls and questions to a friend's feed
· Nothing appears on your feed until you approve it
· Send anonymously if you like
· Share a link and friends can send photos without signing in

■ Friends in orbit
· Your closest friends and groups orbit around you
· Friends with new photos glow brighter
· Swipe between galaxies: Blue (your people) and Pink (trending now)

■ Candids, one photo at a time
· Swipe through friends' and groups' photos in full screen
· Like, comment, and decorate with animated stickers
· Share a favorite photo with a link

■ Shared group albums
· Trips, weddings, clubs — collect everyone's photos in one place
· Public or private groups; join with a single link
· Owners can pin photos, manage members and close uploads

■ Easy uploads
· Pick from your library in-app and drag to reorder
· Zoom, move and rotate inside a 9:16 frame
· Trim videos to the 10 seconds you want

■ Safe by design
· Public or private feed
· Report and block posts, comments and people — even anonymous senders
· Content reported by several people is hidden automatically and reviewed within 24 hours
· Delete your account anytime in the app
