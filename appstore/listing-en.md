## Description
candid. is a photo app where your friends fill your feed — not you.

■ Your feed, filled by friends
The real you, through your friends' cameras, instead of selfies you picked.
· Friends send photos, videos, polls and questions to your feed
· Nothing appears until you approve it
· Share a link and friends can send without signing in
· They can even send anonymously

■ Our feed, filled by just us (private groups)
An album only your people can see — family, best friends, your club.
· Only invited members can see it, and everyone adds photos
· Invite with one link and they're in
· Owners can pin photos, manage members and close uploads

■ Everyone's feed, filled by everyone (public groups)
Albums anyone can fill together — events, trips, show-off-your-dog.
· Anyone can look around, and members post together
· Discover trending public feeds in the Pink galaxy

■ Plus
· Your friends and groups orbit around you, and sparkle when new photos arrive
· Swipe through photos one at a time; like, comment and decorate with animated stickers
· Zoom and rotate inside a 9:16 frame, and trim videos to the 10 seconds you want

■ Safe by design
· Choose who sees your feed and who can send to you
· Report and block — even anonymous senders
· Content reported by several people is hidden automatically and reviewed within 24 hours
· Delete your account anytime in the app

Contact: contact@candid-feed.com
